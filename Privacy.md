# Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Kodoneri is a Safari extension that lets you chat with AI about the pages you visit. This policy explains what data Kodoneri handles and where it goes.

Kodoneri has no ads, analytics, or tracking, and we never sell your data.

## What is sent to AI providers

When you send a message, Kodoneri sends it to the AI provider of the model you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek). It also sends what the AI needs to answer, such as text and screenshots of the pages you ask about. Each provider handles this data under its own policy:

- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- [DeepSeek](https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html)

## With your own API keys

- Your messages go directly from Safari to the provider. They never pass through our servers.
- Your API keys are stored in your iCloud Keychain and sync across your devices.
- Conversations and memories are stored on your device and in your private iCloud database, and sync across your devices. We cannot access them.

## With a Kodoneri plan

- Your messages pass through our relay server to the provider. We do not store or view their content, and payload logging is turned off in the gateway we use (Cloudflare AI Gateway).
- To bill your plan, we store your Sign in with Apple identifier, your App Store purchase records, and the token count and cost of each request. Cloudflare also keeps request metadata, such as the model, token count, and time.
- When you sign in with Apple, Kodoneri does not ask for your name or email address, so we never receive them.
- Conversations and memories are stored on your device and in your iCloud, just as with your own API keys.

## Debug logs

Kodoneri keeps a debug log on your device to help fix problems. The log does not include message text, page content, or URLs. It is shared only if you copy it and send it to us.

## Deleting your data

To delete conversations and memories, delete them in Kodoneri; the deletion reaches iCloud and your other devices. To delete everything Kodoneri keeps in iCloud, remove its data in your device's iCloud storage settings. To delete your plan account data, contact us.
